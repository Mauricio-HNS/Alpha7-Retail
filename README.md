# Alpha7 Retail

AI-powered purchasing, inventory and supplier management platform for fashion and lingerie retailers.

## Vision

Alpha7 Retail helps fashion retailers make better purchasing decisions by combining sales, inventory, supplier data and AI-assisted purchasing workflows.

**Stock → Forecast → Recommendation → Approval → Purchase → Audit**

## Core capabilities

- Product and SKU management
- Inventory control
- Sales history
- Supplier management
- Purchase recommendations
- Replenishment forecasting
- Purchase orders
- Approval workflows
- AI purchasing agent
- Audit trail

## Architecture

Alpha7 Retail is a vertical business application. AI agent capabilities are provided by the Alpha7 AI platform and integrated through explicit tools, policies and controlled actions.
